How to play

    
The Lawn: walk with the arrow keys, WASD or the on-screen pad. Bump into a red number to fight. Battles block the path, so beat them in order. Some plants join you after certain battles. Read the signs, and keep an eye on the hedges...

    
Battles: your team (up to 5 plants) fights waves of zombies. Every round, everyone acts once, fastest first. On a plant's turn, pick a move (click it or press 1 to 4), then click a glowing card if it needs a target.

    
Sun: the whole team shares one sun bank. You start with 50 and get 15 more every round; Sunflower makes 25 more on each of its turns. Basic attacks are free. "Soak up sun" (5) skips a turn for 15 sun.

    
Damage = the move's power x the plant's attack / 5. Armor and shields soak damage first, unless a move pierces. Damage types matter: Wal-nut takes less from blunt hits (but not from zombie bites, which are sharp), Squash's blunt hits get past half of an enemy's armor.

    
Leveling: winning a battle gives XP to every plant you own, even the ones on the bench, and the plants that fought get 25% bonus XP on top (losing gives none). Plants learn new moves at levels 3, 6, 8 and 10 (stats never change). On the Lawn plants can reach level 6; later areas raise the limit. Each plant brings 4 moves; pick them on the Team screen (T). Plants heal fully after every battle.

    
Tip: click any card in battle to see what it does.
